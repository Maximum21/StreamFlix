Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. This policy describes what the StreamFlix app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What the app does

StreamFlix is a movie discovery app. It shows information about films from The Movie Database (TMDB) and plays official trailers through YouTube. It does not stream films, show ads or sell data.

When the app loads film information and posters, your device contacts TMDB and its image servers, which receive your IP address as part of any web request. Trailers are played by YouTube's embedded player, which is subject to Google's privacy policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Your choices

  • Analytics is off until you answer the prompt, and you can change your answer under Settings → Privacy at any time.
  • Notifications are off until you turn them on under Settings.
  • Delete your account under Settings → Delete account. This removes your profiles, My List, watch history and the account itself.
  • Clear cache under Settings removes the film data stored on your device.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Crash and analytics data are retained by Firebase according to its standard retention settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. A "kids profile" only limits what the app shows inside an adult's account.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, access to your stored data is restricted to your account by server-side rules, and backups of app data are disabled.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated. Questions can be raised through the issue tracker of the project's GitHub repository.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
